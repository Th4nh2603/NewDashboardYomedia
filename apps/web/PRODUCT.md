# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are YoMedia internal teams working in authenticated daily operations: creative operations staff, account and admin staff, and technical operators or developers. They manage brands, campaigns, demo links, documents, client-facing creative assets, integrations, file workflows, AI/RAG tools, users, roles, permissions, and operational system data. Future client or brand users may receive limited access, but internal YoMedia workflows are the first priority.

## Product Purpose

NewDashboardYomedia is an internal creative operations dashboard for managing demo and ad creative assets, browsing and organizing files and documents, using AI chat and RAG-backed answers to support work, administering users and permissions, and reducing manual coordination across creative, demo, document, and system workflows. Success means internal teams can move between dashboard, demo management, files, documents, AI assistant, brands, users, and settings quickly without losing context or relying on scattered manual processes.

## Brand Personality

The product should feel professional, fast, focused, practical, and modern without becoming flashy. It should communicate operational confidence and creative competence: a working tool for teams who need clarity, speed, and trust more than spectacle.

## Anti-references

The interface should not feel like a generic SaaS admin template, a flashy AI toy, a chatbot-first playground, a dense enterprise back-office system, an overly colorful marketing website, or a complicated dashboard overloaded with widgets.

## Design Principles

- Prioritize real working flows over presentation.
- Keep navigation clear across creative assets, demo management, files, documents, AI assistance, brands, users, and settings.
- Preserve focus and speed for daily internal use.
- Make AI and RAG feel like practical workflow support, not the center of the product.
- Use modern polish in service of clarity, consistency, and trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target accessible internal-product defaults: readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion accommodations, understandable loading and empty states, and clear error messages that do not expose sensitive backend details. The product should support both English and Vietnamese UI contexts where existing localization patterns are present.
